Five Signs Your HR Processes Have Outgrown Your Organisation

What worked at 30 people will actively paralyze an organisation at 150. Spot the operational warning signs that your informal HR methods are dragging down company growth.

Merit & Beyond Advisory Council

People Operations Practice

September 2026•6 min read

Early-stage agility thrives on informal trust, direct founder access, and ad-hoc problem solving. However, as headcounts surpass critical milestones—50, 100, 250 employees—processes that once felt nimble begin to buckle under administrative weight.

Sign 1: Founders Are Still Approving Leave and Expenses

When executive leadership is required to sign off on everyday operational requests, delegation of authority has broken down, creating an executive bottleneck.

Sign 2: Inconsistent Policies Across Regional Offices or Teams

If employee entitlements, probation extensions, or notice period rules vary depending on which manager is asked, the organisation faces severe legal exposure and employee mistrust.

Sign 3: Heavy Dependence on Offline Spreadsheets Alongside Your HRMS

If payroll inputs and leave balances are manually reconciled in disconnected Excel files every month, your software is not serving as a single source of truth.

Sign 4: Onboarding Consists of Filling Forms with No Cultural Orientation

New hires who spend their first week chasing IT equipment and deciphering unwritten rules take twice as long to become commercially productive.

Sign 5: Exit Interviews Reveal Recurrent, Unaddressed Manager Friction

When top talent departs for reasons that were preventable, it signals that employee relations channels and supervisor coaching are missing in action.

Summary & Executive Follow-Through

Recognizing these signs is not an admission of failure; it is the natural consequence of business expansion. Transitioning to structured, auditable HR processes protects leadership focus and preserves cultural health.
